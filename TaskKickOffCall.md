# Latch - Branding & Logo Kickoff Call

**Participants:**  
**Sarah - PM**  
**Daniel - Creative Director**  
**Alex - Founder**  
**Maya - Co-Founder**

---

**Sarah - PM:**  
"Since we've already gone through the VideoAsk, I think we're good on the basics. Today we mainly want to dig into the branding and logo and understand what you want us to explore."

**Daniel - Creative Director:**  
"Yeah. One thing that stood out to me was the name Latch. Is there a particular meaning behind it?"

**Alex - Founder:**  
"Yeah. We liked the idea of a latch because it's about controlled access. You can open something when you're supposed to, and otherwise it stays closed. That's basically what we're doing with software access."

**Maya - Co-Founder:**  
"But we don't want a literal lock or latch logo. We'd rather have something a bit more clever."

**Daniel:**  
"Got it. So there's a concept there, but we don't necessarily have to show it literally."

**Alex:**  
"Exactly."

**Daniel:**  
"You mentioned Ramp and Attio as references. What specifically do you like about them?"

**Maya:**  
"Ramp feels very confident and polished. Attio feels organised and modern. Neither feels overly corporate."

**Alex:**  
"Yeah. We'd like Latch to have that same confidence, but trust is probably even more important for us because we're dealing with access to company systems."

**Daniel:**  
"So trustworthy, but not traditional security."

**Alex:**  
"Exactly. No shields, padlocks, hackers, dark cybersecurity stuff."

**Daniel:**  
"Would you be open to us exploring a stronger visual concept around access, control, connection or things opening and closing?"

**Maya:**  
"Yeah. Definitely."

**Alex:**  
"Actually, we'd prefer that over just having a nice logo."

**Daniel:**  
"Perfect. Then we'll explore the logo as part of a bigger visual idea, rather than designing the logo in isolation."

**Sarah:**  
"And for the brand guide, we'll show how that idea can extend into the wider identity."

**Maya:**  
"Yeah, that's what we'd like."

**Daniel:**  
"Great. I think we have enough to start."

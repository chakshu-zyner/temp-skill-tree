# Latch - VideoAsk Transcript

## 01. Describe your company

> "So Latch is an access management platform. Basically, we help companies manage employee access across all the different software they use.
>
> As companies grow, you end up with so many tools — Slack, Google Workspace, GitHub, AWS, Figma, Salesforce, Notion, all of that. And every employee obviously needs a different set of access depending on what they do.
>
> The way it's usually handled today is pretty manual. Someone joins the company, IT has to figure out what they need, create all the accounts, give them the right permissions. And then when someone leaves, you have to go back and remove everything.
>
> Latch connects with all those systems and automates that. So you can set things up based on someone's role, and Latch handles the access for them.
>
> And then there's the visibility side too. You can see who has access to what, which is obviously important from a security perspective.
>
> So yeah, at a high level, we're trying to make access management much easier for growing companies."

---

## 02. What problem do you solve?

> "I think the biggest problem is that access management gets really messy as companies scale.
>
> When you're a 20 or 30 person company, you can probably get away with someone just keeping track of things manually. But once you're at 200, 500, maybe 1,000 people, and you've got hundreds of different applications, it becomes a lot harder.
>
> Someone joins and you need to figure out everything they need access to. Someone leaves and you need to make sure everything gets removed. Someone changes teams and suddenly their permissions need to change as well.
>
> A lot of IT teams are still doing this through spreadsheets, tickets, Slack, whatever they've put together internally.
>
> And apart from the time it takes, there's obviously a security risk. You might have someone who left three months ago and still has access to something.
>
> So we're really trying to remove that manual work and give companies a much better way of managing it."

---

## 03. Who is your ICP?

> "We're mainly targeting companies between about 100 and 2,000 employees.
>
> We're especially interested in B2B SaaS and technology companies because they tend to have a lot of different tools and a lot of employees who need access to different systems.
>
> The person we'd usually be selling to is the Head of IT or an IT Manager. Sometimes it might be someone in security, depending on how the company is structured.
>
> I think the sweet spot is a company that's growing pretty quickly. Maybe they started with 30 or 40 people and everything was easy to manage, but now they're at 200 or 300 and suddenly the old way of doing things isn't really working anymore.
>
> They're not necessarily looking for some huge enterprise security platform. They want something that can handle the complexity without making their own lives more complicated."
